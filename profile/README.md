# 👋 Welcome to GeziraPal

We're a small team of students from the **[University of Khartoum]([url](https://www.uofk.edu/))** building products at the intersection of AI, data, and real world problems in the Arab and African context.

We move fast, ship things, and care deeply about building technology that is actually useful, not just technically impressive.

## What We're Building

### [GeziraPal](https://github.com/Gezirapal/GeziraPal)
An AI-powered crop and fertilizer recommendation system for smallholder farmers. Give it your soil and climate readings, it tells you what to grow. Built with TensorFlow and deployed on Streamlit.

> [!NOTE] 
> Named after Al-Gezira (الجزيرة) — Sudan's agricultural heartland.
